Onboarding Service should have tables like:
OnboardingInstance
id, workflowId, status (PENDING, RUNNING, COMPLETED, FAILED)
context (JSON: app details, owner, choices from UI)
StepInstance
id, onboardingId, stepId, status
externalExecutionId (Harness execution id)
startedAt, completedAt, resultPayload, error


The service:
Loads the workflow definition.
Creates OnboardingInstance and StepInstance rows.
Periodically or reactively evaluates “which steps are now runnable”.

{
  "meta": {
    "messageId": "uuid",
    "type": "idp.pipeline.started",   // or idp.onboarding.step.completed
